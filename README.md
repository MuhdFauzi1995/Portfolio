### Movie Magic
#### Overview
The movie industry is unpredictable in terms of each movie's performance. Their budgets are usually in the millions but their success is affected by many factors, such as the genres, the time of release and if that movie belongs to a franchise. The comparison between a movie's box office and ratings is also unclear. The objective of this project is to develop a ETL pipeline by extracting data from The Movie Database (TMDb) though its Application Programming Interface (API). The data is to be transformed into a suitable format and loaded into a SQL Database for analysis to answer various business questions regarding what makes a movie successful. The results from the queries are to be visualized to show and compare the factors affecting a movie's success.

#### Business questions
1. Which release month brings in the highest average revenue?
2. Do franchises earn more than original standalone movies?
3. Are movies getting longer nowadays?
4. Which genres are the most profitable?
5. Does a large box office indicate high movie ratings?

#### Tools used:
- Python
- PostgreSQL

#### Limitations
- The data extracted from the TMDb API is small with only 60 movies extracted.

#### Results
- It was found that movies make the most money in December and January, which are considered holiday months.
- Original movies are as successful as those from franchises.
- The duration of movies do not appear to be longer; they tend to vary over the years.
- The top 3 grossing genres are Romance, Science Fiction ana Drama.
- There is no clear correlation between a movie's box office performance and its ratings.
